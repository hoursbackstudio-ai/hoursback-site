About the Hours Back Pipeline app

"Hours Back Pipeline" is a private production tool used only by the Hours Back channel owner. It signs in to the owner's own YouTube account (through Google's OAuth sign-in) to upload the channel's own videos, set their thumbnails and captions, and read the channel's own performance analytics. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
