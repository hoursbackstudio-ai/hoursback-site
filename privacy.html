Privacy Policy

Hours Back Pipeline · Effective 23 November 2026

Who this applies to

"Hours Back Pipeline" (the "app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Hours Back YouTube channel (youtube.com/@HoursBackOfficial). It is used only by the channel owner to manage their own channel. It is not offered to, and does not collect data from, the public or any other YouTube users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses, and why

After the channel owner signs in with Google, the app may access the following for the owner's account only:

  • Upload and manage videos (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl): to upload the channel's own videos and Shorts, set their titles, descriptions, publish schedules, thumbnails and captions.
  • View analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): views, watch time and audience retention for the channel's own videos, to decide which topics to make next.
  • View monetary reports (yt-analytics-monetary.readonly): estimated revenue for the channel's own videos, once the channel is monetized.

The app also reads public YouTube data (titles and view counts of publicly trending videos) to identify popular topics. It never downloads or reuses other creators' videos.

How data is stored

  • The sign-in token and any analytics reports are stored only on the channel owner's own computer.
  • No data is stored on any server operated by the app, and the app has no user accounts or tracking.

Sharing

Data obtained through YouTube API Services is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page or security permissions page. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored data; stored analytics are deleted within 30 days of revoking access.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact email listed on the Hours Back channel's About page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
